64 built-in looks, all from original recipes

These are not vendor-published LUT files. They are original colour recipes written to match the response of the named styles: cinema, film, black and white, warm/cool, vintage, airy, plus Kodak, Fujifilm, Ricoh, Panasonic, Leica and Hasselblad-inspired grades.

Updated 2026-11-19

Every look has an adjustable strength, two looks can be stacked (a film base plus a slight highlight tint, for example), and the stack can be baked into one .cube for other software. The built-in pack is 33³, and it is the same data as the downloadable cubes.

How to use them

Open the editor in the browser, drop in a photo, and switch looks in the filmstrip along the bottom; the vertical rail sets strength and DUAL LUT opens the second slot. To use the same grades in other software, download all 64 as .cube files (MIT, no signup); for vendor LOG LUTs, the LUT sources page covers where the camera makers publish theirs.

If you are unsure whether LUT precision matters, the measured LUT precision write-up has the numbers.

Try it in the browser

Open a RAW, grade it, stack a look, export a JPEG — all on your machine, with no upload and no account.
