A darkroom that runs in the browser

LUT ARCHIVE does RAW decoding, development and LUT grading entirely in the browser. There is no server-side rendering, no account, and the photo never leaves your device.

Updated 2026-11-19

It exists for one specific situation: you have a RAW, you are on a machine that is not yours, you do not want to install a whole desktop application for one photo, and you would rather not upload it to a cloud drive or an online converter. Open the page, drop the file in, develop it, export it.

Pipeline

The RAW is decoded in a Web Worker: flashraw compiled to WebAssembly handles unpacking, AHD demosaicing, camera white balance and the camera colour matrix. The decode keeps 16 bits and four stops of scene headroom above white, then re-encodes the frame as scene-linear half floats for the GPU. The display transform is darktable's sigmoid, applied per channel with hue preservation and a closed-form parameter fit. Highlights can be recovered because that data was never discarded during decoding.

Exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, whites and blacks in the develop panel are all linear operations in scene-linear space, and the preview and the export share the same pipeline. At 100 % the export renders the in-memory full-resolution frame in tiles, so the JPEG matches what was on screen.

LUTs

The 64 built-in looks come from the original recipes in scripts/looks.mjs, baked to 33³ cubes and packed as per-row deltas plus gzip: 1.34 MB for the whole pack, decoded per look on demand. The same looks ship as standard .cube files in the repository, ready for Resolve, Premiere or Photoshop. They approximate the named film stocks and camera profiles; they are not vendor-published LUT data.

Limits

  • Single-image processing: no library, catalogue, batch apply or cloud sync.
  • The preview decodes at 4096 px on the long edge; export is not capped.
  • WebGL2 is required. Old hardware, or a browser with hardware acceleration disabled, gets an explanatory page instead.
  • Lightroom .xmp presets are not supported; .cube LUTs are.
  • The first RAW open downloads about 3.4 MB of decoder, cached by the browser afterwards.

Open source

The project is MIT-licensed and the source lives at github.com/shisongsong/lut-studio. Change whatever is wrong, or open an issue. The privacy details are in the privacy policy, and the processing steps are in the RAW guide.
